# AUREL — Brand Kit

Source of truth for every phase after this file. Nothing downstream invents a
brand decision; if something is missing here, it is answered here first.

---

## 1. Brand name

**AUREL**

Eau de Parfum. Luxury niche fragrance house with three expressions.

## 2. Slogan

> **Composed in shadow, worn in light.**

## 3. Positioning

AUREL sits in the niche-luxury tier: a small house that composes three
fragrances instead of fifty, each built around one idea and sold at a single
price. Not a department-store launch, not a novelty. It competes on
craft, restraint and the quality of what is in the bottle — concentration,
raw materials, and a bottle made to be kept.

The promise is quiet recognition rather than projection: a scent noticed up
close, and remembered after the wearer has left the room.

## 4. Target audience

- Men and women, 28–50, who have moved past wearing what everyone else wears.
- Buyers who already know the difference between eau de toilette and eau de
  parfum and will ask about concentration before price.
- People who buy one good bottle a year and use it deliberately.
- Gift buyers at the top of the market who want the object to feel expensive
  in the hand.
- Sceptics of loud, sweet, mass-market fragrance; drawn to woods, resins,
  smoke, iris and night-blooming flowers.

## 5. Brand personality

**Refined, mysterious, restrained, cinematic, tactile, assured, nocturnal.**

AUREL speaks quietly and never oversells. It assumes the reader already has
taste. Copy is short, declarative and specific: facts about the material,
then one image. No exclamation, no superlatives stacked on superlatives, no
urgency tactics. The house is certain of itself, so it does not raise its
voice.

## 6. Color palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background (tinted, warm black) | `#0E0A07` | page canvas, never pure black |
| Surface | `#171009` | panels, footer, cards |
| Surface 2 | `#201710` | raised insets, spec rows |
| Text | `#F4EDE4` | headings and body, warm off-white, never pure white |
| Muted text | `#A6968A` | captions, secondary lines, labels |
| Primary accent (gold) | `#C9A24B` | CTAs, active states, rules, small glows only |
| Secondary accent (rouge) | `#8E2434` | rare highlights: Sombre marker, one underline |
| Border / line | `rgba(201,162,75,.20)` | hairlines, card edges |

One strong accent — gold — used sparingly. Rouge appears at most twice on the
page. The canvas is tinted toward the footage (warm amber-black), never
`#000000` or `#FFFFFF`.

**Deliberate exception:** AUREL Élan is a daylight composition. Its section is
allowed to run lighter — sand, ivory, sea light — while Noctis and Sombre stay
in shadow. The three variants are visually distinct on purpose; do not flatten
them into one dark treatment.

## 7. Typography

- **Heading:** Cormorant Garamond — 300, 400, 500, plus 400 italic for accents.
  High-contrast serif with real character. Never Inter, never Roboto.
- **Body:** Jost — 300, 400, 500. Geometric sans, quiet, wide-set.
- **Mono (optional):** ui-monospace for spec values only.
- Wordmark: Cormorant Garamond, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.38em`, weight 400.
- Load only the weights used, with `preconnect`.

## 8. Logo direction

A pure typographic wordmark: **A U R E L** set in tracked uppercase Cormorant
Garamond, with a small gold lozenge (a rotated square) sitting either above
the initial letter in the favicon or as a separator in the footer. No emblem,
no crest, no illustration. The mark must read at 16 px.

## 9. Product / service description

**AUREL Eau de Parfum — three expressions, 100 ml.**

Shared construction across the collection:

- **Concentration:** eau de parfum, 18 % aromatic compound.
- **Volume:** 100 ml, heavy-based flacon.
- **Bottle:** squared smoked-glass flacon with a weighted base and a thick
  glass floor; brushed gold-plated collar; press-fit cap with a soft magnetic
  seat; fine-mist atomiser, 0.09 ml per actuation.
- **Materials:** glass, gold-plated brass collar, magnetised cap, paperless
  lacquered carton.
- **Wear:** 8–10 hours on skin; 6+ hours on fabric.

The three variants:

- **AUREL Élan** — the daylight expression. Pale green-gold liquid. Marine
  woods, jasmine, bergamot, sun-warmed stone. Cut grass and salt air over a
  clean cedar base. Light, lifted, worn from morning.
  *Top: bergamot, sea salt · Heart: jasmine, fig leaf · Base: marine woods,
  cedar, white musk.*
- **AUREL Noctis** — the night expression. Amber-gold liquid. Smoked amber,
  tonka, labdanum, orris, warm resin. Candlelight on dark marble.
  *Top: cardamom, pink pepper · Heart: orris, tonka · Base: amber, labdanum,
  benzoin.*
- **AUREL Sombre** — the shadow expression. Oxblood liquid. Bulgarian rose,
  oud, patchouli, black amber, drifting incense. The deepest of the three.
  *Top: saffron, black pepper · Heart: rose absolute, incense · Base: oud,
  patchouli, black amber.*

Sombre is the face of the hero film — the same bottle, rose, driftwood and
smoke that run through `assets/videos/aurel-hero.mp4`.

## 10. Key benefits (feature cards)

1. **18 % concentration** — eau de parfum strength, not diluted. One application
   in the morning still reads at dinner.
2. **Three compositions, no filler** — a house of three fragrances, each built
   around a single idea instead of a catalogue.
3. **A flacon made to keep** — weighted smoked glass, gold-plated collar,
   magnetic cap. Designed to be refilled, not discarded.
4. **Raw materials you can name** — rose absolute, oud, labdanum, marine woods.
   No mystery blend, no filler aromachemicals.
5. **Eight to ten hours on skin** — measured wear time, stated plainly.
6. **Two atmospheres, one house** — Élan for daylight, Noctis for night,
   Sombre for the hours in between.

## 11. Visual mood

Low-key lighting: a single warm key from behind and to the side, gold rim
light tracing the edge of the glass, everything else falling into warm shadow.
Materials carry the frame — glass reflections, the depth of the liquid, the
brushed grain of the metal collar, condensation, drifting smoke. Environment
is a sophisticated dark interior: black stone, aged timber, marble, a soft
amber haze. Motion is slow only: a camera that pushes in and settles, mist
that drifts, light that shifts across the cap. Camera style is a shallow-depth
portrait lens on a dolly — never handheld, never fast.

Élan breaks this deliberately: daylight, sea, ivory linen, jasmine, pale stone.
Its section is brighter by design.

## 12. Website goal

**One action:** the visitor sends an enquiry — a discovery request or a stock
question — to the house. Everything on the page walks toward the CTA card with
the collection price and the enquiry form. Secondary goals are reading the
three variants and understanding the concentration and materials.

## 15. Hero video brief

The film is supplied and final: `assets/videos/aurel-hero.mp4`. It is not
generated, and it is not replaced.

- **Subject:** the AUREL Sombre flacon — squared smoked glass, oxblood liquid,
  brushed gold collar, standing on wet black stone with a red rose, split
  driftwood and scattered petals, warm backlight through drifting smoke.
- **Camera move — the reveal.** One continuous take, no cuts. It opens in
  **extreme macro**: the squared glass, the depth of the oxblood liquid and
  the brushed gold collar fill the frame, the cap running off the top edge. It
  then **pulls back continuously**, the flacon shrinking toward centre as the
  black stone, driftwood, rose and drifting smoke enter the frame, settling
  into a composed wide shot. Verified frame by frame at t = 0, 2.5, 5, 7.5 and
  9.9 s.
- **Scroll agreement:** scrolling down reads as *pulling back to reveal*. The
  environment arrives rather than the subject escaping, and the film ends at
  rest, so the last frame is a stable place for the page to land.
- **Lighting:** low-key, single warm key from behind, gold rim light on the
  glass edge, deep amber shadow. The accent colour appears as physical metal
  and reflected light, never as flat UI colour.
- **Material detail:** glass refraction through the liquid, the brushed grain
  of the collar, water beads on the rose petals, wet stone specular.
- **Calm space:** at t = 0 the flacon owns the centre of the frame, so the
  **left third** is the calm zone: dark driftwood, rose in shadow, receding
  depth. Hero copy is left-aligned over a left-weighted scrim and is gone
  before the pull-back has revealed the whole bottle. The right side carries
  warm light and smoke and never takes text.
- **Final frame:** wide, still, bottle centred with generous margin above and
  below, smoke at rest, rose lower-left. This is where the page comes to rest.

Verified: 1280×720, 10.005 s, 24 fps. Frame-difference at the tail falls from
~0.5–1.1 to 0.02–0.19, so the ending genuinely comes to rest. No trim required.

## 16. Hero film plan

- **Concept:** the reveal — extreme macro of the materials pulling back to the
  whole flacon in its environment. Already shot; read off the film itself
  rather than generated.
- **Format:** A — one continuous supplied take. No stitching, no per-section
  clips, no external generation service anywhere in the build.
- **Storyboard:** (1) extreme macro of glass, oxblood liquid and gold collar,
  cap off the top edge — where the hero copy sits over the dark left third;
  (2) the pull-back that brings the whole flacon, rose, driftwood and stone
  into frame — lands behind the composition and materials chapters; (3) the
  settled wide rest — lands at the collection, CTA and footer.
- **Resting frame:** the final wide shot. Extracted as `public/img/ending.jpg`
  and reused as the Design & materials backdrop.
- **Text placement:** hero copy is left-aligned in the left third over a
  left-weighted scrim plus text shadow, tuned against t = 0 where the bottle
  is largest. It is fully faded before the pull-back hands the frame to the
  product, so headline and flacon never compete for the same pixels.
- **Scrub mapping:** video time maps to whole-page scroll progress, with the
  two pinned sections acting as slow zones so the film lingers where the copy
  is densest.
- **Phones:** composed still frame (`public/img/poster.jpg`) instead of the
  scrubbing film; the video is never requested on touch or reduced-motion
  devices.
