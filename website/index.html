01 / The composition

Built in three movements.

Every AUREL opens, turns and stays the same way. Top notes for the first minutes. Heart for the next two hours. Base for the rest of the day. We publish the structure, because the structure is the product.

  • Top Bergamot, sea salt, fig leaf
  • Heart Jasmine, orris, crushed stem
  • Base Marine woods, cedar, white musk

02 / The flacon

Weighted glass. A collar you can feel.

100 ml in a squared flacon with a thick glass floor, so it sits down when you set it down. A gold-plated brass collar. A cap that finds its own seat on a magnet. A fine mist, 0.09 ml a pull, enough to cover the collarbone once.

03 / Design and materials

Smoked glass, brushed gold, nothing else.

The bottle was drawn around the liquid, not the other way round. Thick walls so the colour reads deep. A flat floor so it stands on a wet shelf. Metal where the hand touches, glass everywhere else, and a carton with no plastic in it.

  • Nine hundred numbered flacons per expression, then the run stops
  • Lacquered board carton, uncoated, no film, no insert plastic
  • Refills sold separately for the flacon you already own
